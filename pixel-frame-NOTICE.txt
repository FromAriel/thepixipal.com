The reused profile, bitmap/corner, normal, material, composition and lighting
algorithms retain their original source comments. PixiPal replaces the demo's
page-global ownership with isolated module installers and a typed React adapter.
The ImageData constructor is injected for browser rendering and deterministic tests.
